Weekly forts

Fines escalate each week you miss the minimum. The count resets when you meet it again.

The rule. 100 forts per week. This applies during off-season and Home Kingdom only — not during Lost Kingdom.
  1. 1st week missed

    Activity fine

    40M Food40M Wood20M Stone20M Gold
  2. 2nd week missed

    Fine doubles

    80M Food80M Wood40M Stone40M Gold
  3. 3rd week missed

    Major fine

    240M Food240M Wood120M Stone120M Gold
  4. 4th week missed

    Removal from the kingdom

    All resources outside the bag are collected, and you are removed from Kingdom 3922. Refusing to pay ends in a full zero.

Guardians

Guardians are killed on the schedule set by management, so the kingdom keeps the buff window.

  1. 1st offence

    Early kill fine

    40M Food40M Wood20M Stone20M Gold
  2. 2nd offence

    Fine doubles

    80M Food80M Wood40M Stone40M Gold
  3. 3rd offence

    Major fine

    240M Food240M Wood120M Stone120M Gold
  4. 4th offence

    Zeroed, without warning

    No further notice is given at this stage.

Kingdom buff window. Killing a Guardian early while a kingdom buff is active is charged immediately at 100M Food, 100M Wood, 100M Stone and 100M Gold.

Baulur Ironhand

Baulur is killed only with R4 management approval.

  1. 1st offence

    Unauthorised kill

    30M Food30M Wood15M Stone15M Gold
  2. 2nd offence

    Fine doubles

    60M Food60M Wood30M Stone30M Gold
  3. 3rd offence

    Major fine

    120M Food120M Wood60M Stone60M Gold
  4. 4th offence

    Removal from the kingdom

    You are removed from Kingdom 3922 and given 15 days to migrate. Staying past that window ends in a zero.

Alliance Mobilization

The minimum is 1,500 points per event.

  1. Under 1,500

    Mobilization fine

    40M Food40M Wood20M Stone20M Gold
  2. Under 1,200

    Fine and removal from K22V

    The standard fine still applies, and you are moved out of K22V for the reward distribution round.

Low DKP

KVK seats are limited. Governors who sit out a season are asked to make room.

  1. Near the minimum

    Weekly contribution until the next war

    30M of each resource — Food, Wood, Stone and Gold — every week until the next KVK begins.

  2. Far below

    Removal or zero

    Removal from the kingdom, or a zero, with 15 days allowed to migrate.

Leadership orders

Markers, rally calls and direct orders from leadership are followed as given.

  1. Judged case by case

    Penalty depends on the damage done

    Ignoring a marker or an order can cost resources, your place in the kingdom, or your troops, depending on what it cost the kingdom at the time.

Ark of Osiris

Registering takes a slot from someone else. If you sign up, you show up.

  1. 1st no-show

    No-show fine

    30M Food30M Wood15M Stone15M Gold
  2. 2nd no-show

    Fine doubles

    60M Food60M Wood30M Stone30M Gold
  3. 3rd no-show

    Banned from Ark, kingdom-wide

    You can no longer join Ark of Osiris with any Kingdom 3922 alliance, on top of the fine below.

    120M Food120M Wood60M Stone60M Gold

Rules protect the people who show up

Fines exist so that active governors are not carrying the ones who aren't. Meet the minimums and none of this page applies to you.
