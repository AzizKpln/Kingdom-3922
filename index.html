Kingdom Regulations

Penalties & Fines

Violations may result in resource fines, alliance removal, kingdom removal or zeroing depending on severity and repeated offenses.

Kingdom 3922 rules are designed to protect active players, maintain fair participation and ensure that kingdom resources and leadership efforts are respected. Repeated violations may result in progressively stronger penalties.
